# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Veterinarians and livestock technicians managing cattle health, reproduction, and milk production at agricultural operations in Peru. They work in a clinical/operational mindset — each record is consequential, not exploratory. They expect precision from their tools and have low tolerance for ambiguity. They are competent domain experts, not tech-first users.

## Product Purpose

Zootech is an enterprise livestock management SPA for Peruvian agricultural operations. It consolidates cattle registry, milk production logging, health triage, reproduction cycle tracking (heat detection and insemination), and client/tenancy management into a single operational tool. Success means a vet can open any animal's record, log a health event, and navigate back to the dashboard without friction.

## Brand Personality

Reliable · Precise · Professional. The tool earns trust through accuracy, not aesthetics. Data is primary. The interface should disappear into the task. When something is wrong, users need to know immediately — not be left guessing.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS cream: warm beige/sand backgrounds, gradient text, large hero metrics — the AI-default 2025 aesthetic
- Dark-mode dev tool: terminal-native black, monospace accents — feels like a developer product, alienates agricultural professionals
- Legacy agri ERP: dense gray Bootstrap tables, zero visual identity, accounting software from 2010 — the thing Zootech is replacing

## Design Principles

1. **Data over decoration.** Every visual element serves information or action. Decoration that doesn't communicate something must not exist.
2. **Earned familiarity.** UI patterns follow established conventions (tables, forms, modals, sidebars). Users trust what they recognize. Surprise without purpose erodes confidence.
3. **Precision at scale.** The interface handles hundreds of animals, years of records, multi-user environments. Design for density without sacrificing clarity.
4. **Errors are critical, not cosmetic.** In a clinical context, a missed validation or unclear status can mean wrong treatment. Feedback must be immediate, specific, and actionable.
5. **Trust the professional.** No hand-holding, no patronizing empty states, no tutorial overlays. These users know their domain. The tool should match their expertise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Target users may be working outdoors on tablets or shared desktop workstations under varied lighting conditions. Color contrast must hold in bright ambient light. Touch targets must be adequate for tablet use.
